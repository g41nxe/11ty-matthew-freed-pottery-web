# Context: Matthew Freed Pottery

## Glossary

### Market
A recurring market Matthew sells at, such as "Trout Lake Farmer's Market". Name, location, hours, directions and description are the same every time; only the dates change. A market is listed on the events page under the market schedule with all its upcoming dates.

### Event
A one-off or multi-day occasion that is not a market, such as the Harmony Arts Festival or a studio opening. An event has its own date, optionally an end date, and may take place at the studio. On the website events appear under "Special events".

### Date
One day on which a market takes place. A market holds a list of dates; an event has a start date and optionally an end date.

### Studio news
The single news entry shown in the "Latest from the studio" box on the events page.

### Shop set
One group for the "From the current firing" section on the home page: a featured piece plus up to four shop items, usually from one glaze collection. The home page shows one set at random on every visit; a hidden set stays out of the rotation.

### Placement
Where on the website a shop link sits, written into its address as `utm_content` when the site is built — for example `firing-tofino-tile2`, `home-teaser-yaletown` or `nav`. It names the spot, not the piece: Shopify already knows the piece from the page the visitor lands on, and a spot keeps its name when a piece is renamed.
