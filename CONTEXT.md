# Context: Matthew Freed Pottery

## Glossary

### Market
A recurring market Matthew sells at, such as "Trout Lake Farmer's Market". Name, location, hours, directions and description are the same every time; only the dates change. A market is listed on the events page under the market schedule with all its upcoming dates.

### Event
A one-off or multi-day occasion that is not a market, such as the Harmony Arts Festival or a studio opening. An event has its own date, optionally an end date, and may take place at the studio. On the website events appear under "Special events".

### Date
One day on which a market takes place. A market holds a list of dates; an event has a start date and optionally an end date.

### Studio news
The single news entry shown in the "Latest from the studio" box on the events page.

### Shop set
The pieces of one glaze that come first on its glaze page, named after that glaze. Everything else beside them comes from the shop, so a set says "these before the others", not "only these". A set holds up to four pieces and no photo of its own (ADR 0006).

### Group photo
The large picture on a glaze page, beside the pieces: several pieces of that glaze together, no price, no single product behind it. It belongs to the glaze, not to its pieces, so a glaze can have one without having anything in the shop. Until Matthew has taken one, the glaze's card image stands in its place.

### Glaze page
One page per glaze, built from the glaze's own intro and page text: name, text, the pieces from the shop with their group photo, the band asking for a commission, and four other glazes. A glaze without both texts gets no page, and its card keeps linking straight to the shop.

### Placement
Where on the website a shop link sits, written into its address as `utm_content` when the site is built — for example `firing-tofino-tile2`, `home-teaser-yaletown` or `nav`; a shop link Matthew writes into a text field gets `text-` plus the page, like `text-events`. It names the spot, not the piece: Shopify already knows the piece from the page the visitor lands on, and a spot keeps its name when a piece is renamed.
