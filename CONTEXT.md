# Context: Matthew Freed Pottery

## Glossary

### Market
A recurring market Matthew sells at, such as "Trout Lake Farmer's Market". Name, location, hours, directions and description are the same every time; only the dates change. A market is listed on the events page under the market schedule with all its upcoming dates.

### Event
A one-off or multi-day occasion that is not a market, such as the Harmony Arts Festival or a studio opening. An event has its own date, optionally an end date, and may take place at the studio. On the website events appear under "Special events".

### Date
One day on which a market takes place. A market holds a list of dates; an event has a start date and optionally an end date.

### Studio news
The single news entry shown in the "Latest from the studio" box on the events page.

### Shop set
A hand-picked group of pieces for the "From the current firing" block on the home page, which shows one set at random per visit. A set no longer reaches into a glaze page: there the shop alone decides which pieces stand and in what order (available ones first). A set holds a featured piece and four tiles (ADR 0006).

### Group photo
The large picture at the top of a glaze page, beside the name and text: several pieces of that glaze together, no price, no single product behind it. It belongs to the glaze, not to its pieces, so a glaze can have one without having anything in the shop. Until Matthew has taken one, the glaze's card image stands in its place.

### Glaze page
One page per glaze, built from the glaze's own intro and page text: name and text beside the group photo, the row of pieces, the band asking for a commission, and a slider of the other glazes. The row holds every piece of the glaze the shop has for sale (sold-out ones only fill up to four), then the market tile and the next market date. With two pieces or fewer that makes four cards, which stand as a fixed row with a placeholder for each piece missing; with more the row is a slider. A glaze without both texts gets no page, and its card keeps linking straight to the shop.

### Placeholder
A white tile in the row of pieces on a glaze page, with three dogwood flowers in the glaze's colour, fading from left to right, and one line below it where a piece carries its name. It fills the row up to four cards where a glaze has fewer than two pieces in the shop. Two of them fade as one row of six flowers, and only the first carries the line.

### Market tile
The tile on a glaze page that says the glaze can also be seen at a market. It stands at the end of the row of pieces on every glaze page, followed by the next market date. Its text is the same whether the shop holds none of the glaze or a dozen pieces.

### Placement
Where on the website a shop link sits, written into its address as `utm_content` when the site is built — for example `firing-tofino-tile2`, `home-teaser-yaletown` or `nav`; a shop link Matthew writes into a text field gets `text-` plus the page, like `text-events`. It names the spot, not the piece: Shopify already knows the piece from the page the visitor lands on, and a spot keeps its name when a piece is renamed.
